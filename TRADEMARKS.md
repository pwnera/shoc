# Trademark policy

The **shoc** name and logo are held by the project's maintainers. The code is
Apache 2.0 and you may do as that licence allows; the name is not covered by it.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your product, service or blog post **uses**, **is built on**, or **is
  compatible with** shoc.
- Use the name in truthful descriptions, talks, articles and comparisons.
- Redistribute unmodified builds under the name.
- Fork the code, publish the fork, and say it is **derived from** shoc.

## What needs permission

- Naming a product, company, service or domain in a way that suggests it is the
  shoc project, or endorsed by it, for example "shoc Cloud", "shoc Pro",
  "shoc-hosted".
- Using the logo as your own mark, or in a modified form.
- Selling a hosted service under the shoc name.

## What we ask of forks

Fork freely; that is what the licence is for. Please rename the distribution so
that users can tell whose software they are running and who supports it, and
remove the logo. Keeping the `NOTICE` file and the attribution satisfies the
licence.

Questions, or a request: **trademarks@shoc.dev**.

*The trademark registration is still in progress; this policy describes our
intent either way.*
